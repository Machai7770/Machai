Context of the game

You just arrived back home from a long day of school. You have a lot of school work to finish and you are very overwhelmed. However, you do have choices that you can make to relieve your stress. What you do with yourself determines the outcome of the game. Your objective is to finish all your task you have set for the day. Keep in mind of your hunger bar and pressure bar. those objects are very important for you to complete your task.

Locations

    • Living Room
    • Outside
    • Bedroom
    • Kitchen
    • Bathroom

Possible endings

There are multiple endings to this game. The ending you get is based on the choices you make throughout the game. Make sure to manage your time wisely and complete your tasks before time runs out.

  • Ending 1: You finish all your tasks and go to bed on time. You wake up feeling refreshed and ready for the next day.
  • Ending 2: You finish some of your tasks but not all. You go to bed feeling stressed and overwhelmed.
  • Ending 3: You don't finish any of your tasks and go to bed feeling defeated and unaccomplished.
